# Product screenshots

Captured from the public demo project (`https://app.monoscope.tech/p/00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000`) with `scripts/capture-product-shots.mjs`. Viewport 1440×900 at 2x (2880×1800 WebP, `cwebp -q 80 -m 6 -sharp_yuv`; rows not marked "(recaptured)" are from the first capture and used `-q 86`), timezone UTC. Every shot exists as `<name>-dark.webp` and `<name>-light.webp`; the theme is set with the app's `theme` cookie. All shots are full-viewport (no crop) and keep the sidebar and header.

The script hides the demo-only "Book Demo" / "Start Free Trial" header buttons, chat widgets and toasts. Refresh everything with `node scripts/capture-product-shots.mjs`, or refresh single shots with `node scripts/capture-product-shots.mjs trace issues`.
