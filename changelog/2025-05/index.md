## AI SRE

- *Ask in plain English.* Type a question into the explorer, using the Ask AI button or a `?` prefix, and monoscope writes the query for you.

## Logs & Traces

- *New query editor.* Autocomplete, popular queries and suggestions based on the fields your project actually sends.
- *Richer queries.* Use duration units (`duration > 500ms`, `duration > 1.3s`), decimals and `in (...)` lists.
- *Charts.* A visualization editor shows any query as a chart as well as a list.
- *Zoom.* Drag across a chart to zoom into that time range. The time picker follows.
- *Facets.* The facets panel scrolls and resizes, remembers which boxes you ticked, and rounds large counts.
- *Columns.* Hide columns and drag them into a new order, with a marker showing where a column will land.
- *Details drawer.* Resize the event drawer. Its width is remembered between visits.
- *Errors stand out.* Every span with an error shows an error badge in the list.
- Filter by field works on every field, and live streaming adds new rows more smoothly.

## Dashboards & Metrics

- Add charts with custom queries to a dashboard, duplicate widgets, and copy a chart from the explorer onto a dashboard.
- Dragging and resizing widgets works better.

## Onboarding & Billing

- *Free plan limit.* The free plan now includes 1,000 events a day, and projects that go over see a clearer message explaining the limit.
- *API keys.* The API keys page has Active and Archived tabs, show and copy buttons, and lets you reactivate a revoked key.
- The billing page, the time zone picker in project setup and the side navigation were fixed and tidied.

## Performance

- The log list is faster, and new results appear sooner.
