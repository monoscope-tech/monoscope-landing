## Anti-patterns verdict

Detector: local source+rendered = 0 findings; prod rendered = 5 (4 pastels + em-dash). Console: ConvertKit embed throws appendChild TypeError on every load; grid.svg preload warning ×9.

## Local vs production
Better local: title/OG (prod's are empty), 0 eager videos (prod 9 ≈ 78MB), 0 eyebrows/rainbow-hexes/side-stripes/h5s (prod 16/34/2/22), 900 hero vs ~600, trial-primary nav (prod inverts), closing differentiator band, detector-clean.
Local loses: product proof invisible — poster-less 5–22MB videos paint as empty blue boxes where prod shows real screenshots instantly; double ending (new dark band + old footer CTA, different button sets); quote-card links now external Trustpilot vs prod's (misdirecting) "case study" labels.
