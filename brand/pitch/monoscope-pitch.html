Cost estimate on page 9

We based these estimates on September 2026 public list prices, with annual billing where it’s offered and 30-day retention for every vendor.

Workload: 50M events/month, 5 hosts, 2,000 metric series and 5,000 replay sessions. Each event averages 2 kB. Each metric series sends one data point per minute.

Datadog: Infrastructure costs $15/host and APM costs $31/host. Log ingestion costs $0.10/GB. Indexed logs cost $2.50/million. Custom metrics cost $5/100 series beyond 100/host. Per 1,000 sessions, RUM costs $0.15 and Session Replay costs $2.50.

Splunk: Observability Cloud App & Infrastructure costs $60/host. Splunk Cloud log ingestion costs about $12.50/month per GB of daily ingestion. RUM costs $14/10,000 sessions. This estimate excludes custom metrics.

Sentry: Team costs $26/month. Spans cost $1.60/million beyond 5M. Errors cost $0.29/1,000, with an assumed error rate of 0.5%. Replays cost $3.75/1,000. This estimate excludes metrics.

monoscope Cloud: $29/month includes 20M events, 20M metric data points and 2,000 replay sessions. Additional usage costs $1/million events, $1/10M metric data points and $1/1,000 replay sessions.
