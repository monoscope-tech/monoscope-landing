Verification

Passed:
- node brand/booth/printer-v2/build.mjs: generated six full-size vector PDFs,
  six SVG originals and panel previews without errors.
- pdfinfo on all six PDFs: one page each; media dimensions match the stated
  millimetres at 72 points per inch, within PDF metadata rounding.
- pdffonts on all six PDFs: no font objects; lettering is vector outlines.
- pdfimages -list on all six PDFs: no raster images embedded.
- Source guard: no SVG text or image elements in any print panel.
- pdftocairo -png -singlefile -scale-to 1800 on backdrop PDF: inspected
  rendered result; complete copy, no truncation, no line before left bracket.
- pdftocairo -png -singlefile -scale-to 1400 on front PDF: inspected rendered
  result; centered original mark and exact monoscopehq.com address.
- Resvg previews: inspected the backdrop, front, side graphic, and combined
  proof. Side nodes are centered on the path. Panel graphics are independent.
- Digital WCAG luminance-ratio calculation: solid navy #000626 on white
  19.92:1; white on blue #0068FF 4.75:1. Both exceed the 4.5:1 normal-text
  reference. These are source-color checks, not print accessibility claims.
- Colors are valid sRGB hex values; no P3 or out-of-sRGB colors introduced.

Not verified:
- APCA was not measured; this package uses WCAG ratios as a digital reference.
- Physical fabric color, lighting, stretch, viewing distance, seams and
  hardware fit require the supplier's imposed proof and material sample.
- PDF/X, CMYK output intent and bleed are intentionally not supplied without
  the printer's profile/template. See START-HERE.txt for production handling.
- Screen-reader, keyboard, loading, error, responsive and dynamic-value tests
  are not applicable to the printed panels and were not claimed.

Verdict

Approve — static artwork handoff. Manufacture only after supplier template
mapping and a size/color proof, as specified in START-HERE.txt.
