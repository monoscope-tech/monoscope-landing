PRINTING INSTRUCTIONS
Use the PDFs as the artwork masters. Do not print the PNG previews.
All text and logos are vector outlines; no fonts need to be installed.
PDF page size equals the finished visible panel size. Print at 100% / 1:1.
There are no crop marks, added bleed, seam allowances, or hidden annotations.
These are panel-face artwork files, not a stitched fabric cutting pattern.

Before manufacture, map the panels to your actual hardware/cloth template.
Add your required bleed, seams, hems, zipper and tension/stretch allowances.
Extend the blue background into the table seam/bleed areas.
Extend white around the backdrop and top panel as needed.
Keep all critical copy and logo artwork inside the visible safe area.
Side trace graphics are independent; no front-to-side line alignment is
intended. Do not rotate or mirror the supplied side panels accidentally.

If the tabletop is already white, panel 05 does not need printing.
Panel 06 is optional for a wrap with a closed back. An open back can improve
staff access to storage. Choose the construction with the booth supplier.
