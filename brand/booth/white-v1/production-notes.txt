MONOSCOPE — WHITE BOOTH / V1

Concept dimensions (confirm against the supplier's templates):
Backdrop: 3000 × 2400 mm.
Table front: 1800 × 950 mm.
Table sides: 750 × 950 mm each.
Table top: plain white, 1800 × 750 mm.

The SVG files are editable vector artwork at full nominal size. All lettering
is outlined, including the original Monoscope logo. PNG files are viewing
previews only. artwork-proof.png shows the front panel over the backdrop;
this is a composition preview, not a physical placement or fabrication plan.

These files contain the visible face graphics, without bleed, seam allowance,
back panels, zipper placement, or tabletop printing. A supplier must adapt
them to the actual hardware and cloth pattern before production. Table sides
are separate compositions; do not assume their traces align across seams.

Colors: navy #000626, electric blue #0068FF, white #FFFFFF (RGB).
Ask the supplier to convert using their fabric/printer ICC profile and supply
a color proof. Blue fabric should be checked in particular. Keep critical
text clear of folds, hardware, and seam areas after applying the template.

Suggested materials: matte tension fabric backdrop and fitted fabric table
wrap, with a plain white tabletop. Confirm material and dimensions with the
supplier before ordering.
